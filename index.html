Method

How a plate is made.

Three steps stand between a city and its place on your wall - the same sequence, for every plate in the atlas.

I.

The city is drawn

An AI-created map composition captures the real street grid, parks, rails and water in the atlas palette.

II.

The plate is refined

Each artwork is reviewed and corrected by hand, then numbered, titled with its local name and set with exact coordinates.

III.

The print is made

Plates are prepared at archival resolution and printed on demand via Redbubble, in the size and paper you choose.
